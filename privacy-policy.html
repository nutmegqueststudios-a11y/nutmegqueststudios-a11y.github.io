Privacy Policy

Nutmeg Quest Studio · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the games published by Nutmeg Quest Studio ("we", "us") handle information about you. It applies to every game we publish, including TimeRunner, on any app store, unless a game's store listing points to a different policy.

In short:

  • Our games do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress, settings and scores are saved on your own device. We do not receive them.
  • Some games show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads, and asks for your consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for our games and we do not collect personal information ourselves. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, postal address or photos;
  • your contacts, messages, files or precise location;
  • account logins, because our games have no accounts.

Information stored on your device

Each game saves its own data on your device, such as levels completed, upgrades, scores and settings. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game, and most of our games also offer a "Reset Save Data" option in their settings.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

2. Third-party services in our games

Some of our games use services from other companies. These companies may collect information automatically when the game runs. Each game's store listing (for example the "Data safety" section on Google Play) shows which of these it uses.

If we add another service to a game, we will update this policy and that game's store listing before the change is released.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, our games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Personalised ads: you can turn off personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.

4. How information is used

Information collected by the services above is used to:

  • show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance;
  • prevent fraud and keep the services secure;
  • find and fix crashes and performance problems.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising relies on your consent. Non-personalised ads, security and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests in keeping our games free to play and safe.

5. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it. If we ever publish a game designed for children, it will follow the stricter rules that apply to children's apps and will say so on its store listing.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about players, most requests are best handled:

  • in the game, through Privacy Options (ad consent) and Reset Save Data (your saved progress);
  • on your device, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID or uninstalling the game;
  • with Google, for data collected by its services, through Google's privacy controls.

You can also contact us at the address below and we will help. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents

We do not sell personal information for money. Ad identifiers shared with advertising partners may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You can opt out by turning off personalised ads in your device settings as described above.

7. Keeping and protecting information

Game saves stay on your device until you delete them. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies. Data sent between our games and these services is encrypted in transit. Google may process information in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we release a new game or add a service. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change significantly affects how information is handled, we will also mention it in the affected game's update notes.
